(c) Magnesium is a metal. List two general physical properties of metals (think shine, conductivity, malleability...).

Question 20Compare 3 Forces
The three main non-contact forces are gravity, the electric force, and the magnetic force.

(a) Name one similarity all three share. (Hint: how they decrease over distance, or the existence of a "field," etc.)

(b) Name one major difference between gravity and the other two. (Hint: gravity can only do one thing — pull. Electric and magnetic forces can do something else too.)

(c) Give one real-world example for each of the three forces.
