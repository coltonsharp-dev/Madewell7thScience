Three sections · 20 questions total
SECTION A · Multiple Choice · 14 pts
14 questions covering cell theory, prokaryotic vs eukaryotic cells, organelle functions (nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplast, ribosomes, cell membrane, cell wall), the photosynthesis equation, and levels of organization. Click an option to select it — you can change your mind until you submit.
SECTION B · Fill-in-the-Blank · 4 pts
Four short fill-ins on key vocabulary: ATP, photosynthesis, cell wall, lysosome. Spelling counts but minor variations are accepted (e.g. "photo synthesis" = "photosynthesis").
SECTION C · Short Response · 2 pts
Two open-response questions: (1) compare a plant cell with an animal cell (3 shared, 2 plant-only); (2) trace the path of energy from sun to a running animal using all 8 listed terms. Use complete sentences.
How to complete & turn in
1
Click Open Day 1 Worksheet above — it opens in a new browser tab.
2
At the top, fill in Last Name, First Name, your period, and today's date.
3
Section A: click the option you think is correct for each of the 14 multiple choice questions. The selected option highlights in mint.
4
Section B: type your one-word (or two-word) answer in each fill-in box.
5
Section C: type your full short response into the textarea. Label parts (a) and (b) where asked.
6
Scroll to the bottom and click Copy My Answers. The button copies all 20 answers + how long you spent on each question to your clipboard.
7
Return to this Canvas page → click Submit Assignment → Text Entry tab → paste (Ctrl+V or ⌘V) → Submit Assignment.
⏱
The worksheet records how long you spend on each question (based on whether the question is on screen) and includes those times in your submission. Don't speed-scroll — actually read each question.
Grading
Section A · Multiple Choice (1 pt each) 14 pts
Section B · Fill-in-the-Blank (1 pt each) 4 pts
Section C · Short Response (4 pts each) 8 pts
Total 26 pts
Multiple choice and fill-ins are objectively scored. Short responses are graded on accuracy and use of correct vocabulary. Time-on-question data is informational — used to flag students who may need re-teaching, not to penalize.
Expectations
  • Work independently. No copying. Looking through your notes is allowed.
  • No partial submissions. All 20 questions must have an answer before you copy.
  • Use complete sentences on Section C.
  • Spelling counts — but obvious typos won't get you marked down on fill-ins.
  • If something breaks (browser closes, etc.), re-open the file and start over. Submissions are not saved between sessions.
