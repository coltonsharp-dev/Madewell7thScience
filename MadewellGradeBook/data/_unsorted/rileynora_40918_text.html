The periodic table is helpful to organize elements. This is because it provides many ways that the elements can be organized. These include: atomic number, period, regions, and atomic mass. On a peiodic table, elements are labled in small boxes with their symbol, name blow their symbol, atomic mass below their name, and atomic number in the top left corner. For example, on sodium, you have NA in the middle, sodium below it, 22.990 below that and 11 in the top right corner. Elements are also catagorized by where they are on the table and what color it is. For example, iron, a metal, is towards the center of the table and on the table given, it is a mustard yellow, meaning that it is part of the metal catagory.
